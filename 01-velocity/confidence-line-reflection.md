# Confidence Line Reflection (optional)

> Module 1 · Velocity. Toy vs tool, and where this build moved you on the Confidence Line.

Reflection after Lab 1 and the first peer swap. Revisit after the Lab 2 upgrades and second peer review.

## Toy or tool?

The build helped me identify a product question: what context does someone need to judge whether a recommended action follows from an insight? My peer understood the intended connection and expressed conditional interest in an actionable dashboard, but could not tell whether this was the right action to take. The prototype generated a useful learning; the recommendation is not yet supported enough to guide a user's decision.

## Confidence Line placement

I am still at the high-ambiguity end of the Confidence Line. I built a first screen, showed it to a peer, and learned that clarity of layout does not establish the logic of a recommendation. My next iteration will make that reasoning visible and test whether a new peer has enough context to judge the action. The sample data and one peer's conditional interest do not validate the underlying product assumption.

## The feedback that landed hardest

“are the things really related? or just presented together”

I need to show the connection between evidence and action. I will keep the screen concise, explain why the action is worth testing, and label the proposed explanation and intervention as hypotheses.
